All photos are from Wikimedia Commons, downloaded 2026-11-06 as the 1920 px wide version. Changes: the files in
res/drawable-nodpi are cropped to 1920x1080 (16:9) and darkened at the top and bottom edges and a little overall (done
once by tools/BakeWallpapers.java), re-encoded as JPEG (quality 90), and wallpaper_*_thumb.jpg is a 96 px square of the
middle of the photo. The unchanged downloads are in wallpaper-originals/ (Misty Trees as _AdobeRGB-original.jpg; its colour
profile was Adobe RGB and is converted to sRGB by the tool, so the TV needs no colour conversion).
Because of the CC BY-SA licence, these changed copies of the photos are shared under the same licence as the originals
(CC BY-SA 4.0 or 3.0 for the photos marked so, CC BY 4.0 for the two marked CC BY). The MIT licence in LICENSE covers the code only.
